# **App Name**: PinHigh

## Core Features:

- Score Entry Hub: Log your rounds with a simple interface capturing the date of play and your total score, featuring a persistent record for your history.
- Smart Club Selection: Easily select golf courses from a searchable database, with 'Seletar Country Club' set as your default for lightning-fast recording.
- Intelligent Course Assist: Uses a tool to intelligently retrieve handicap ratings and slope information whenever you play a new or unfamiliar course to ensure score accuracy.
- Dynamic Score Adjustment: Calculates and displays an adjusted net score based on the current course handicap versus the default Seletar baseline.
- Session History: A scrollable timeline of all your recorded scores to track your golfing progress over time with persistent cloud storage.
- Performance Analytics: Visualize your score trends and handicap improvements over time with interactive charts.
- Handicap Index Tracker: Automatically calculate and update your official handicap index based on your recent rounds.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary Color: Deep Emerald Green (#1DBF54) for links and brand accents.
- Background Color: Obsidian Moss (#1A231D), a dark, desaturated shade for a luxurious digital feel.
- Accent Color: Electric Lime (#A5F274), used sparingly to draw attention to score buttons and statistics.
- A pairing of 'Space Grotesk' for bold headlines and 'Inter' for highly legible body text and scorecard data.
- Clean, focused single-page dashboard designed for one-handed data entry on the move.
- Smooth, subtle slide transitions when switching golf courses to signify the shift in context.
- Minimalist, thin-stroke icons representing golf equipment and course features.
